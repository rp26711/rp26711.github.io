Module: Deciphering Big Data

My academic journey, learning, reflections and projects throughout the module.

Welcome to my e-portfolio, which presents my work and reflections from the Deciphering Big Data module, undertaken as part of my PGCert in Data Science. Developed throughout the 12-week module, this portfolio documents my achievement of the expected learning outcomes, personal development, and professional reflections on each element of the module.

    Learning outcomes:
  • Identify and manage challenges, security risks, limitations and opportunities in data wrangling.
  • Critically analyse data wrangling problems and select appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques for preparing, cleaning, exploring, optimising and evaluating big data.
  • Design, develop and evaluate data-processing solutions to solve complex problems across different environments using relevant programming paradigms.
  • Develop the skills needed to contribute effectively to development teams in virtual professional environments, understanding real-world roles and organisational practices.
