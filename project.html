Project Aim

As the project progressed, my understanding of the relationship between data quality, database design and analytical reliability developed significantly. I recognised the importance of data processing and cleaning in improving the validity and reliability of analysis (Huxley, 2020). Working with a real-world dataset demonstrated that technical insights are only as reliable as the data and decisions underpinning them. Challenges in interpreting and preparing the data encouraged me to adopt a more critical approach to methodology and the credibility of the resulting findings.

The use of AI, particularly ChatGPT, was another significant learning experience. Although AI can improve efficiency and provide valuable support with coding and technical problem-solving (Hao et al., 2024), the project reinforced that its outputs cannot be assumed to be accurate and human oversight and judgement remains essential. The Human–AI–Human approach, which maintains human agency throughout the AI integration process, was particularly relevant to my practice (Giray, 2025). Reviewing, testing and validating AI-generated outputs strengthened my understanding of responsible AI use.

Overall, the project developed my technical confidence, independence and critical thinking. Navigating challenging team dynamics also strengthened my ability to adapt, communicate effectively and respond constructively to changing circumstances. The most significant lesson was that successful data projects require more than technical implementation. They require careful planning, critical evaluation, reflection and justification of decisions.
