Professional Development

Professional Skills Matrix and Module Action Plan


To support my progression throughout the module, I developed a professional skills matrix to evaluate my existing competencies and identify areas for development across professionalism, ethics, collaboration, technical skills and conceptual application. This enabled me to identify skills gaps and establish priorities for my learning.

My subsequent action plan prioritised technical and conceptual development, reflecting my aspiration to pursue a career in engineering rather than analysis. I recognised the importance of combining practical proficiency with a sound contextual understanding, which I developed through hands-on use of tools, experimentation and trial and error, supported by theoretical knowledge. The matrix also prompted me to consider ethical implications that I might otherwise have overlooked, which proved particularly relevant throughout the module.

Reflecting on this process, I found that the matrix accurately represented my abilities while broadening my awareness of development needs. Revisiting it throughout the module provided a reference point for evaluating my progress and identifying areas for improvement. Furthermore, documenting my objectives in an action plan encouraged more deliberate planning and greater ownership of my learning.

I would adopt this approach in future academic and professional development, as it provided a structured means of setting priorities and evaluating progress. This reinforced the importance of balancing technical competence with contextual understanding and ethical awareness in my future engineering career.
