Collaborative Discussion 1

This collaborative discussion in Weeks 1 - 3 strengthened my critical understanding of IoT, including its opportunities, limitations, security risks and data-cleaning challenges. Reviewing academic literature enhanced my research and critical thinking skills, while peer collaboration broadened my perspective on preventative data cleaning, design-stage planning and continuous data governance. Tutor feedback further encouraged me to consider contemporary trends in data sovereignty and regulatory compliance, which I had overlooked during the discussion. This highlighted the growing importance of evolving regulatory frameworks in ensuring data quality and responsible technology adoption. Overall, the activity demonstrated how collaborative learning and critical reflection can deepen understanding and inform effective data management strategies.

    • Collaborative Discussion 2

    This collaborative discussion in Weeks 8 - 10 enhanced my understanding of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), particularly its requirements for protecting personal data and ensuring appropriate security measures. By researching the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) guidance and reviewing academic literature, I improved my knowledge of data protection principles, compliance requirements, exemptions and the responsibilities organisations have when handling personal information. Peer collaboration enabled me to compare interpretations of GDPR, explore different perspectives and learn from others’ research and experiences. Providing feedback encouraged me to consider practical measures for preventing data breaches and reducing security risks. Overall, the activity strengthened my legal awareness, critical thinking, research and communication skills, helping me understand how GDPR supports responsible data management and protects individuals’ privacy.
