Engaging in peer collaboration allowed me to exchange ideas, consider alternative perspectives and learn from others’ experiences and interpretations. Providing constructive feedback helped me identify potential solutions to data security issues and consider preventative measures. Overall, this activity strengthened my ability to critically evaluate data wrangling challenges and identify appropriate strategies for managing risks effectively.
